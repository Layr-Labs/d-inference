### Nemotron Super durable prefix checkpoints

- The managed Nemotron 3.5 Super slot keeps its exact text prefix reuse as complete encrypted SSD checkpoints by default, like the other cache-enabled models: KV, Mamba conv/SSM state and the MTP assistant's prompt history at native precision, bound to the published artifact and prompt-contract identity. Checkpoints survive provider restarts within the store's TTL, the slot advertises a durable capability (`prefix_cache_v2_models`) so the coordinator can keep durable holders, and the coordinator's repeat hint gates writes against the daily SSD write budget. The 16 GiB resident bank is no longer reserved by default; `DARKBLOOM_PREFIX_CACHE_MEMORY=1` selects it instead (it remains the only tier that reuses image turns), and `DARKBLOOM_PREFIX_CACHE=0` turns both off.
