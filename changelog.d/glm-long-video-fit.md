### GLM-5.3-Flash shrinks a long video's frames instead of refusing it

- GLM-5.3-Flash refused, with 400 `Invalid media input`, any video whose 2 FPS sample at its own size passed the processor's 512 MiB prepared-pixel cap (28,532 visual tokens): 720p past about 24 seconds, 1080p past about 11. Its 240,000-token video budget never bound first, so the official resize never shrank the frames. In production these were about 1% of GLM's vision 400s (121 on 11-13, 10–20 a day since), all inline videos from OpenRouter.
- The video budget a GLM Mac and a GLM pair's leader load is now that cap, so the official resize shrinks every frame of such a clip to fit, as it shrinks any frame past the budget: a 26-second 720p clip renders at 1204 x 700. A clip within the cap renders exactly as before. The decoded-frame cap still bounds a clip's length at its own size (about 97 seconds of 720p, 43 of 1080p), and all media in one request still share the prepared-pixel cap.
- `DARKBLOOM_GLM_LONG_VIDEO_FIT=0` (or `false`, `no`, `off`) keeps the artifact's own budget and the refusal; `darkbloom start` passes it to the launchd provider.
