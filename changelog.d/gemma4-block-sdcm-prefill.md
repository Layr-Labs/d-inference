### Gemma 4 Block-SDCM prefill, zero-deletion coherent mode, and paged last-query prefill

- Added `CBv2LastQueryPrefillLayerCache` conformance to `PagedLayerCache`, enabling exact layer-29 last-query prefill on paged Gemma 4 26B deployments (matching the contiguous backend).
- Added opt-in Gemma 4 26B prefill acceleration (`DARKBLOOM_GEMMA4_PREFILL_SDCM=coherent|lightcone|balanced|fast|turbo`, default `off`) for solo cold text prompts at or above `1536` tokens:
  - `coherent` (`fullkv`/`full`): zero-deletion full-KV prefill keeping 100% of prompt tokens in all 5 full-attention KV caches (`L5`/`L11`/`L17`/`L23`/`L29`), dense `L23`, intact `L28`, `k=2` interior MoE with exact top-8 on the trailing `1024` tokens, sparse `L5`/`L11`/`L17` attention, and norm-matched interior sliding-layer skipping on `L24`–`L27`.
  - `lightcone`, `balanced`, `fast`, `turbo`: Block-Atomic Causal-Adjoint Multigrid (`Block-SDCM`) with dual-scale frontier (`F8+F64`) + quota-partitioned scale-free reciprocal-rank Reverse Causal Prefix Resonance scoring, contiguous trailing-window (`W=1024`) retention, `2W/L` adaptive short-prompt retention floor on `lightcone`/`balanced`, optional post-Layer-0 Tri-Signal pre-compaction (`turbo`), true-window sliding KV commits (`[T - W ..< T]` contiguous, `origPos >= T - W + 1` compacted), position-aware `O(L * W)` Prefix+Local+FullTail sparse L5 attention, energy-matched early MoE top-k with exact softmax renormalization, exact original-position RoPE, chunked banded sliding-window attention, and decoupled `positionDelta` logical KV offsets across paged/contiguous/standalone caches.
- Hardened CBv2 integration with capability-gated scheduling (`supportsCompactedPromptKVWrite` / `supportsCompactedPositionDelta` / `supportsApproximatePrefillProvenance` / `supportsCompactedPromptPrefill`, refusing quantized paged KV storage, step-owned paged pools, frozen-replay rows, and `.off` models fail-closed), `.evaluationOnly` partial-chunk protection, sticky `usedApproximatePrefill` prefix-cache donation veto, and per-request `disablePrefillCompaction` veto automatically applied on `LoopExitThinkingContinuation` retries.
- Recorded optional configured `prefillSDCMMode` provenance in `SchedulerPrefillBenchmarkReport` when `DARKBLOOM_GEMMA4_PREFILL_SDCM` is configured.
- Limitation: Gemma 4 prefill acceleration is opt-in (default `off`), applies only to solo cold text prefills on non-KV-shared Gemma 4 MoE models with unquantized paged/contiguous KV storage, and disables durable exact prefix-cache donation for approximate-prefill requests.
