### Join the cluster and Darkbloom Sandbox waitlists from the CLI; sandbox setup waits for approval

- `darkbloom cluster waitlist join [--macs 2-4]` and `darkbloom sandbox waitlist join` join the waitlist that Darkbloom approves accounts from, with this Mac's chip and memory and the `darkbloom login` token (`POST /v1/waitlists/{feature}` on the coordinator of `provider.toml`). `darkbloom cluster waitlist` and `darkbloom sandbox waitlist` show the status and place in line; `--json` prints the coordinator's answer.
- A first `darkbloom sandbox setup` now checks that Darkbloom approved the account for Darkbloom Sandbox before it checks or installs anything. Not on the waitlist: setup offers to join it (`--yes` joins); waitlisted, revoked or not logged in: setup says so and exits 1. A coordinator without waitlists (404) lets setup go on, and a setup that continues, repairs or updates an installed add-on does not ask.
- The cluster commands are unchanged: `darkbloom cluster waitlist` records the request, and `darkbloom cluster` does not check it.
