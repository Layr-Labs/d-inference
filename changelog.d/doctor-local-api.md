### `darkbloom doctor` knows how this Mac serves you

- The report opens with a `LOCAL API` section: how this Mac serves (Only me, Darkbloom network or Both, me first; what runs now, else your saved choice), its local API's address, whether it answers and on which port, whether its key file is there (the key is never shown), and whether OpenCode and Hermes point at it, or somewhere else. Every problem names the next command: `darkbloom start`, the free port to use, `darkbloom restart`, or `darkbloom --opencode` / `--hermes` to point a tool here again.
- On a Mac set to Only me, `doctor` no longer checks what Darkbloom serving needs: the account link, MDM, coordinator health and trust, App Attest, attestation readiness, coordinator traffic and billing are skipped, with one line that says they aren't needed. `--coordinator <url>` still runs them. `darkbloom verify` is unchanged.
- On a Mac in a two-Mac cluster, the section names the pair's way of serving and the Mac that runs the pair's API.
