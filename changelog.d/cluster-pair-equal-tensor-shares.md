### Qwen3.8 27B pairs of two different Macs register with the coordinator

- A pair set up with `darkbloom cluster` to serve through Darkbloom is now planned with equal tensor-parallel shares. On two different Macs the planner used to give the faster Mac the larger share of every layer (for example 2:1). The coordinator registration cannot name unequal shares yet, so each Mac's part refused to register ("The saved roster registration is not valid for this Mac"), the coordinator never formed the pair, and it received no routed requests, although its status said it was offered for pairing. This hit Qwen3.8 27B on any two different Macs, because it pairs only in tensor parallel. GLM-5.3-Flash plans no unequal shares and was not affected.
- A pair that serves you alone (`darkbloom cluster local`) still gets the faster unequal split.
- The leader now refuses a coordinator's approval for a setup saved with unequal shares before it stops either Mac's part. The status says why in one line: `Not offered for pairing: this Mac's check refused it: Unequal tensor-parallel shares serve local clusters only …`. After updating both Macs, if the status says this, press `m` once in `darkbloom cluster` to plan the model again with equal shares.
