### Fewer GPU stalls while serving

- The proactive MLX buffer-cache sweep no longer flushes between busy engines' steps. It waits for five seconds of whole-Mac idleness and then flushes without reporting device activity, so deadline quiescence and idle calibration survive it. While engines serve it flushes only when the reusable pool crowds out admission headroom, and a refused engine-owned KV charge now signals the same shortfall-gated reclaim as request reservations. Cold loads drop that pool and resample before evicting a resident model. `DARKBLOOM_TTFT_IDLE_CACHE_SWEEP=0` restores the old sweep.
- Paged KV pools keep up to an eighth of their slot grant (at most 2 GiB) of free segments across requests and reinstall them for the next admission instead of allocating, zero-filling and fencing new 64 MiB segments. New segments wrap zero-fill-on-demand host pages, so admission no longer waits on a process-wide GPU synchronize. Retained segments are uncommitted (no grant, floor or ledger charge), are dropped on a refused admission or on process memory pressure (any refused charge, a pressure sweep or a macOS memory-pressure event), count as reclaimable for cold loads, which release them before evicting a resident model, and return to the allocator after 30 s of engine idleness. `DARKBLOOM_TTFT_SEGMENT_RETENTION=0` restores release-time trimming and device-filled segments; `DARKBLOOM_TTFT_SEGMENT_HOST_ZEROS=0` keeps retention with device fills.
