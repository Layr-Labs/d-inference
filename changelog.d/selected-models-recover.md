### Selected models recover their residency automatically

- A model you selected explicitly (`enabled_models`, `preload_models`, the pinned `model` or Autopilot's selection) that the startup preload skipped or failed to load for memory, or left untried at the slot cap, is now retried on the capacity tick: after 30 s, backing off to every 10 minutes, at once after any unload, and when a placement hold or an idle threshold ends. Previously nothing retried it, so behind a busy model outside the selection it stayed cold until a restart.
- A retry loads without evicting a selected model. It may first unload only idle models outside the selection (no request in flight, no Autopilot hold or pin, idle for at least two minutes) whose memory makes the room, and it waits while a live Autopilot lease manages residency.
- `darkbloom status` prints `Retrying <model> when memory frees; next check in …` with what blocked the last check, and `Configuration change needs a restart to apply: enabled_models adds/removes …` when the saved filter differs from the one the running daemon started with.
- `darkbloom stop`, `restart` and `start` no longer need `--force` in two states: a graceful drain with the coordinator connection down now finishes once no work is left (after a 10 s grace for a reconnect) instead of reporting a timeout at once, and shutdown waits at most 15 s for each model's unload, so a model whose native retirement never finishes no longer keeps the process from exiting.
- Policy, pending owner review: a selected model an Autopilot operation unloads to place a model outside the selection waits to come back. Once the placement's hold has been over for ten minutes (or, without a hold, ten minutes after the selected model began waiting), an adopted model that keeps it out only by serving requests is released: it takes no new requests, finishes those in flight and is evicted. Holds, selected models and pins are never released.
