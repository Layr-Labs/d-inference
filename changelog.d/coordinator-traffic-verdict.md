### Say why a connected provider gets no coordinator work

- `darkbloom status` adds a `Traffic:` line and `darkbloom doctor` a `coordinator traffic` check. Either the coordinator can route work to this connection, or the line says why it cannot, with a fix: not connected; no coordinator verdict minutes after connecting (previously only `Trust: awaiting coordinator status`); App Attest authorization expired and not renewed; or the coordinator's reason for not authorizing it. Reasons include a locked login keychain, a stalled Apple App Attest call, App Attest unavailable on macOS 26 or outside a logged-in GUI session, and a connection without a linked account (`darkbloom login`). A connected Mac with a warm model no longer looks healthy while it receives nothing.
- The daemon state file records the coordinator connection (`coordinator_connection`: connected, since, connections).
- The provider now re-registers a connection the coordinator has not authorized for 15–30 minutes (a random delay per process), then again after 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours and every 4 hours while it stays unauthorized, and only with no accepted work. A new registration starts a new coordinator verification session, which clears coordinator states that previously needed a restart, such as verification that stopped after an unclassified failure or waits out an hourly retry. `darkbloom logs` shows when the coordinator stops or resumes routing work to the connection and each re-registration.
