### Qwen 3.6 35B ends a looping thought in an answer by default

- `qwen3.6-35b-a3b-vl-mtp-mxfp8` joins the builds whose loop guard ends a repeating thought with `\n</think>\n\n` and continues to the answer (`provenBuilds`), as Qwen 3.5 9B and 35B do since 0.10.13; before, each such thought ended `length` with no answer. On a GPU A/B at OpenRouter's GPQA settings (T 0.5, a 65,536-token cap, 594 draws a side on the signed 0.10.13, plus its loop-prone questions), 14 of the 20 thoughts the guard ended were answered (8 right), with no error and no accuracy difference outside noise. A `DARKBLOOM_LOOP_EXIT_THINKING` list without `qwen3_5_moe` (for example `gemma4,gemma4_text,gpt_oss,qwen3_5`) turns it off along with Qwen 3.5 35B's; `0` turns off every model's exit.
