### The desktop app's Clusters page sets up a two-Mac pair and runs it

- A new **Clusters** page sets up a pair with `darkbloom cluster`'s own steps, questions and words, without Terminal. **Set up a pair** (once your account is approved on the Clusters waitlist, which the page shows) and **Serve on my own Macs only** (no approval needed) open a guided setup: each step as the CLI says it, live work with one row per Mac, copy and download bars, the pairing code large with its Yes or No, and the questions as lists you move through with ↑ and ↓ and take with Enter, such as how the pair serves (Only me, Darkbloom network or Both, me first), the model with its split ("tensor parallel", "pipeline") and how both Macs split it. It ends with the ✓ or ✗ card. **Cancel** asks first while a pairing is in progress, and leaving the page doesn't stop a setup: it's there again when you come back.
- On a paired Mac the page shows both Macs and which one leads, whether the other Mac answers, the model with its mode and each Mac's share, how the pair serves, its state with the CLI's reason and next step, and the cluster service. **Start serving**, **Stop serving** and **Restart serving** act on the pair. **Change model or how it serves** opens the setup on the Mac that leads. **Serve this Mac alone** and **Leave the pair** ask first; leaving says what `darkbloom cluster leave` does.
- This Mac's overview shows the pair in one line, with a link to Clusters.
- With a Darkbloom CLI from before clusters, the page says it needs a newer CLI; with one that can't run the setup from the app, it names the Terminal commands to copy.
