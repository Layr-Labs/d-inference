MoE short candidate parity failures, GPTOSS default-profile failure, host exclusions and interrupted matrices remain explicit; the fresh Qwen9 short comparison is a same-geometry control. These measurements do not establish a deployed-fleet hit-rate increase or a decoder-throughput improvement.
