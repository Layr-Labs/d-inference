# Nemotron Super image ingress measurements — 2026-10-15

> Last updated: 2026-10-15

Golden parity, decoder error, CPU time and peak memory of the Nemotron Super
image decoder and SDK resize, measured on 2026-10-15. These are CPU-only
measurements of decode and preprocessing; no model, vision tower or full
checkpoint ran. The format contract is the
[image format reference](../reference/nemotron-super-image-formats.md).

## Conditions

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Hardware | Apple M3 Ultra (20 performance and 8 efficiency cores), 256 GiB |
| OS and toolchain | macOS 27.2 (26B5091g), Swift 6.4 (swiftlang-6.4.0.34.1) |
| Sources | d-inference `fb5edcc1d` (decoder) and `681c92f5b` (tests); mlx-swift-lm `c86f869c` (resize) |
| Reference | Pillow 12.2.0 (libjpeg-turbo 3.1.4.1, zlib-ng 1.3.1) and numpy 2.3.5 in an isolated venv; CPU PyTorch 2.12.0; transformers 5.7 `load_image` semantics |
| Fixtures | 80 generated by `scripts/nemotron-qualification/image-goldens` (50 PNG, 30 JPEG) |

## Parity

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| PNG RGB8 versus Pillow, with and without orientation | 50 of 50 SHA-256 equal |
| PNG normalized tensors versus PyTorch, full tensors | Bitwise equal for all 50, including a 2560x1440 downscale |
| Previous SDK resize on the same inputs | Up to 1.05e-3 (downscale, at 255→0 steps) and 2.2e-5 (upscales) |
| Existing SDK oracle vectors (6 resize cases) | Previously within 3e-6; now 0 |

JPEG RGB8 error, |ImageIO − Pillow| per sample:

| Fixtures | Maximum | Mean | Maximum off 16-pixel MCU edges |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gray, gray with 2x2 sampling factors | 1 | 0.039 | — |
| CMYK with and without APP14 | 1 | 0.047 | — |
| 4:4:4 baseline/progressive, YCbCr without JFIF, Adobe transform 1 | 3 | 0.126 | — |
| Adobe RGB (transform 0) | 2 | 0.366 | — |
| 4:2:2 | 3 | 0.406 | — |
| 4:2:0 baseline, progressive, ICC, MPO, odd size, restart intervals | 9 | 0.487 | 3 |
| 4:2:0 saturated colour edges | 46 | 0.438 | 3 |
| EXIF/XMP orientation set (4:4:4) | 3 | 0.155 | — |

The 4:2:0 excess is at MCU edges: ImageIO upsamples chroma within each 16x16
MCU and replicates it at the edge, while libjpeg-turbo's triangle filter
interpolates across MCUs. Over full normalized tensors (PyTorch from Pillow's
RGB8) the largest deviations were 0.047 (4:4:4), 0.135 (4:2:0) and 0.672
(saturated 4:2:0 edges; mean 0.006).

## CPU time

Median of 20 runs of a standalone optimized build of the decoder files and the
SDK resize, one thread, under the shared GPU-lane lock. Prepare is decode plus
grid plus resize/normalization; base64, JSON and the vision tower are excluded.
Inputs are synthetic photo-like content; the PNG at 5751x5751 is smoother to fit
the 25 MiB encoded limit. "Before" is the base SDK resize (`1ce44e9a`), measured in
the same session except for four inputs taken from an earlier run the same day.

| Input | Encoded bytes | Target | Decode (ms) | Resize before → after (ms) | Prepare after (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PNG 512x512 | 475,113 | 512x512 | 2.32 | 1.88 → 0.44 | 2.73 |
| JPEG 512x512, 4:2:0 | 53,313 | 512x512 | 0.91 | 1.85 → 0.40 | 1.30 |
| PNG 1024x1024 | 1,898,947 | 1024x1024 | 7.36 | 7.10 → 1.69 | 9.07 |
| JPEG 1024x1024, 4:2:0 | 211,193 | 1024x1024 | 4.33 | 7.08 → 1.71 | 6.08 |
| JPEG 4032x3024, 4:2:0 | 2,448,514 | 2112x1600 | 35.08 | 190.33 → 36.57 | 71.73 |
| PNG 5751x5751 (largest admitted square) | 19,155,370 | 1856x1824 | 219.42 | 403.44 → 90.50 | 309.73 |
| JPEG 5751x5751, baseline 4:2:0 | 6,648,071 | 1856x1824 | 97.80 | 401.51 → 90.40 | 188.12 |
| JPEG 5751x5751, progressive 4:4:4 | 8,764,451 | 1856x1824 | 345.25 | 402.32 → 90.39 | 435.56 |

Container inspection took at most 0.53 ms. Of the 219 ms PNG decode, zlib
inflate took 71 ms.

## Tower working memory

The runner track measured a 2048x1536 fixture request growing MLX peak by
683 MB against a 474 MB media+KV reservation. Media reservations now add the
pinned composition's derived tower and projector bound for the largest image
(`NemotronSuperImageComposition.pinnedActivationBytes`). MLX peak growth was
measured with `Memory.peakMemory` above the evaluated, retained input:

| Case at 104x128 patches | Measured peak growth | Bound or reservation |
|---|---|---|
| SDK, fixture tower (head dim 2, unfused attention, float32) | 753 MB | 1,503 MB bound |
| SDK, head dim 80 (fused attention, bfloat16, width 160) | 226 MB | 298 MB bound |
| Provider, fixture owner over live HTTP, 2048x1664 PNG | 794 MB | 2,556 MB media reservation (461 MB before) |

The pinned bound at 13312 patches is 2,095,272,960 bytes: embeddings
431,565,824, one block 886,712,320 and the projector 776,994,816. The pinned
tower's head dimension, 80, takes MLX's fused attention; its real peak needs the
full checkpoint.

## Limitations

- One machine and OS build. ImageIO may change between OS releases; the test
  bounds keep about a third of margin above these maxima.
- JPEG is not bit-exact; only bounds are enforced.
- 48 MP photographs (8064x6048) are refused by the SDK's 256 MiB resize
  workspace policy; 33 MP squares and 8K UHD frames are admitted.
- No full-checkpoint, tower or end-to-end latency measurement is included.
