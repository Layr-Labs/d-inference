### Provider CI: the MiMo metadata is prepared while the build runs

- Every provider shard and Provider Gates restore the verified MiMo metadata cache and start the pinned preparation (the same three commands, in the same order) in the background before they wait for the test products, instead of after the products. Each job waits for it where the preparation stood; a failed preparation fails the job at that wait, with its output, and, as before, a shard then skips its Swift tests (shard 1 also the public prompt golden vectors). Every file is still checked against its pinned SHA-256.
- A Mac serves only the cache entries it saved, so on the 110 green pull-request runs of 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24 the preparation downloaded the metadata after the products in 246 of 550 shard jobs and 51 of 110 Provider Gates jobs, in 4 to 99 s (median 7 s). Modelled on those runs (not measured), with `BootSecurityServeLoopTests` in its own process and Prompt Parity's downloads during the build, the provider path is 10.4 s shorter per run on average and never longer.
