### Fused causal prompt attention

- Attend a full-attention prompt chunk with one fused causal attention call instead of 128-query blocks when MLX serves its head geometry with a fused kernel: head_dim 64, 72, 80, 96 or 128 on every Apple GPU, and head_dim 256 on M5 (NAX) chips for chunks of at least 1024 tokens. GPT-OSS full-attention layers on every chip and the Qwen3.5/3.8 full-attention layers of M5 solo prefill stripes take the new route; sliding-window, softcapped, vision-span and Gemma 4 layers are unchanged.
- On M3 Ultra, GPT-OSS 20B cold time to first token drops from 19.5 s to 17.4 s at 32K prompt tokens and from 3.41 s to 3.30 s at 8K. On M5 Ultra, Qwen3.5-9B peak memory at 32K prompt tokens drops from 13.86 GB to 12.03 GB because its head_dim 256 layers no longer materialize attention scores.
- GPT-OSS output is bit-identical; Qwen output on M5 can differ within bf16 rounding. `DARKBLOOM_TTFT_FUSED_CAUSAL_PREFILL=0` restores the blocked path in a foreground process; the provider LaunchAgent does not receive it.
