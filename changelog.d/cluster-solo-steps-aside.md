### Single-Mac serving steps aside for a started pair, and the pair goes back to Darkbloom at once

- When the pair is started and wants this Mac's part, but single-Mac serving holds the Mac's serving lock and nobody chose it since the pair was started, the cluster service now stops it the way `darkbloom stop --solo` does. It logs why, and `darkbloom status` says so too. This covers single-Mac serving restarted by 0.10.8's updater during tonight's update, by a login, or by the watchdog. It doesn't step aside when you chose single-Mac serving yourself: `start --solo`, the terminal's "Serve on this Mac alone" and serving alone from the app record that choice, and starting the pair or `stop --solo` on that Mac clears it. A Mac updated from 0.10.8 has no recorded choice, so the started pair wins. It never steps aside while the pair isn't running there (serving alone stays as it is).
- Parts that ended because single-Mac serving held a Mac's serving lock no longer count toward the pair falling back to local-only serving. Once single-Mac serving stops, through the offer, `stop --solo` or a step aside, the count resets. An approval set aside for "parts kept ending" is offered to Darkbloom again at once, instead of after up to an hour.
- When the two Macs run different versions, the start's ✗ says that first, not the single-Mac block.
