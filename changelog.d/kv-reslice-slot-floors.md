### Models load when every model's minimum KV fits

- Loading a model beside others no longer fails with "would leave a model without its fixed request workspace, admission watermark and 1.0 GB minimum KV allowance" while the KV budget still covers every model's minimum. The split gave each model a share in proportion to its per-token KV cost alone. A resident model with a small per-token cost and a fixed per-request workspace, such as Nemotron 3.5 Lightning with its assistant on, could get less than its own minimum and block every further load. Now, when that would happen, each model gets its minimum first and the rest of the budget is split as before. A load is refused only when the minimums together exceed the budget.
- A split that already gives every model its minimum is unchanged. Unloading a model, or a change to the activation reserve, no longer shrinks another model below its minimum.
