### Qwen video: more frames, a timestamp and a vision block per frame pair

- Qwen 3.8 27B, Qwen 3.5 35B, Qwen 3.5 9B and Qwen 3.6 35B now read video as their reference processor (Transformers' `Qwen3VLVideoProcessor`) does: frames sampled at 2 per second, up to 32 a clip (8 before), each frame pair labelled `<t.t seconds>` in its own vision block, and the clip sized as a whole to a budget of 4,096 visual tokens shared by a request's clips (about 1,024 before). The model saw 8 small frames with no times. On 120 VGI-Bench questions with long videos condensed to keyframes, Qwen 3.8 answered 50% instead of 41% (46% with the new layout at the old size), with 2.6 s instead of 0.7 s to the first token on an M5 Ultra. Clips trimmed without re-encoding or starting after an empty edit are read by time. `DARKBLOOM_QWEN_VIDEO_MAX_FRAMES` and `DARKBLOOM_QWEN_VIDEO_MAX_TOKENS` set other values (`8` and `1024` give the earlier size). Two-Mac pairs keep the earlier video layout.
