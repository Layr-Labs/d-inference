## Lifetime, cancellation and cleanup

Preparation/lifetime are anchored once from the coordinator's original absolute
times. Owner launch does not restart them. Checked arithmetic retains one fixed
cleanup deadline. Exact current connection, nonce, epoch and generation apply to
every frame; seen epochs cannot be reused across reconnects. Coordinator cancel
may skip intentionally dropped earlier sequence numbers, and no other type may.

One serialized writer and at most two waiting callers bound public-control
memory. Writer acquisition and network completion respect the deadline. The
existing synchronous Secure Enclave signing API is **not preemptible**; neither
this patch nor its tests claim a bound on that call itself. Cancellation marks
the connection inactive and requests native cleanup without acquiring the writer
or waiting for the signer. A stalled signer can retain a local control obligation;
it cannot publish after invalidation or authorize replacement capacity. This
fail-closed condition requires the signer to return or process-level recovery.

Pending cancellation joins publication before reusing a session. A committed
session remains quarantined unless its actual native terminal, authenticated
owner lease-release ACK, and natural owner exit zero are all observed. Only then
may the same original connection send B's exact release proof. Reconnect cannot
send the old proof through a new connection. The connection shutdown/reconnect
path immediately detaches the native control. Unknown cleanup, failed factory,
missing release ACK or failed publication grants no replacement slot. The
existing owner journal resolution and Process cleanup are reused unchanged.
