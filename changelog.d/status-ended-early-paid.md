### `darkbloom status` says which requests that ended early are paid

- The line under `Requests served` said that every request that ended early "may be unpaid". Many are paid: when the requester leaves mid-answer, the coordinator pays for the tokens your Mac had already sent. The line now says so, and names the two unpaid cases, for example: "1 of them ended early: paid for the tokens sent if the requester left mid-answer, unpaid if another Mac answered first or the connection dropped." The count itself is unchanged.
