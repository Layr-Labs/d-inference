### Speculative decoding keeps each model's own output distribution

- Every model now drafts with exact acceptance unless `mtp_acceptance` or `mtp_acceptance_by_model` says `typical`: each token a speculating sampled request commits is the model's own draw, so its outputs follow the model's distribution (exact in distribution; the tokens are not bit-identical to decoding without MTP). This reverses #1376, which made lossy typical acceptance the default on 10-07 for its decode gains; only the Bonsai 9B candidate and `Qwen3.5-9B` had stayed exact.
- The models that change: Gemma 4 26B QAT (most of its rows draft), GLM-5.3-Flash on one Mac (while one or two requests decode), Qwen 3.8 on Macs (a request decoding alone), Nemotron 3.5 Lightning when MTP is on, and the 35B Qwen 3.5/3.6 builds for requests that send `presence_penalty: 0`. Pairs, native MiMo and Nemotron Super were already exact.
- Typical acceptance keeps drafts the model would rarely have sampled, and once a repetition starts the drafter predicts it and it is kept. In the 0.10.12 gate the catalog Qwen 3.5 builds looped under it where exact acceptance did not (Qwen3.5-9B: 13 of 31 draws against 0 of 31), and on OpenRouter's GPQA runs drafted answers ran to the output cap far more often (Qwen 3.6: 36 % of answers while it drafted, 9 % once it stopped; Gemma 4: 30 %).
- Exact acceptance keeps fewer drafts, so speculative decoding speeds a stream up less (2026-10-07 measurements at one request: Gemma 0.96x, Qwen 3.8 0.93x, Qwen 3.6 0.90x of typical); `mtp_acceptance = "typical"` restores the old rule for operators who accept the trade.
