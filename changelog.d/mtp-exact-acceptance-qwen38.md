### Qwen 3.8 27B drafts with exact MTP acceptance on a single Mac

- The catalog `EigenLabs/Qwen3.8-27B-4bit-mtp` (and its head-less target `EigenLabs/Qwen3.8-27B-4bit`) now uses exact draft acceptance by default: each token a speculating sampled request commits is the model's own draw, so Qwen 3.8's outputs follow its distribution (exact in distribution; the tokens are not bit-identical to decoding without MTP). This applies on one Mac (network serving, Only me / `--local`, and the mid-session MTP upgrade). Qwen 3.8 pairs never used typical acceptance, and every other model keeps its built-in rule.
- On a single Mac a lone Qwen 3.8 request drafts, and typical acceptance keeps drafts the model would rarely have sampled. On Qwen3.5-9B and Gemma QAT that made long answers loop to the output cap, which is why they moved to exact first.
- Exact keeps fewer drafts: a lone Qwen 3.8 request decodes at about 0.93x of typical (the 10-07 measurements), and since only lone requests draft, Qwen 3.8 Macs lose about 1–2 % of decode overall. `mtp_acceptance_by_model` with `"EigenLabs/Qwen3.8-27B-4bit-mtp" = "typical"` restores the old rule.
