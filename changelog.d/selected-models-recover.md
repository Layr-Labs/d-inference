### Selected models recover their residency automatically

- A model you selected explicitly (`enabled_models`, `preload_models`, the pinned `model` or Autopilot's selection) that the startup preload skipped or failed to load for memory, or left untried at the slot cap, is now retried on the capacity tick: after 30 s, backing off to every 10 minutes, at once after any unload, and when a placement hold, an idle threshold, a minimum dwell or a lease ends. Previously nothing retried it, so behind a busy model outside the selection it stayed cold until a restart.
- The same applies to a selected model an Autopilot operation unloads to load another model, for example Nemotron Super unloaded to place GLM-5.3-Flash: it waits from the moment it leaves residency, whether the operation then succeeds or fails, and comes back once the placement no longer protects GLM. An operation that only unloads models queues nothing.
- A retry loads without evicting a selected model. It may first unload only models outside the selection whose memory makes the room and that no request could load straight back: no request in flight, no Autopilot hold, pin or per-model lease, resident for Autopilot's `min_dwell_seconds`, and idle (since its last request or its hold's expiry) for the longer of two minutes and `min_idle_seconds`. A model of the running daemon's selection, even one your saved filter dropped, is never evicted, so the two cannot alternate. It waits while a live Autopilot lease manages residency.
- `darkbloom status` prints `Retrying <model> when memory frees; next check in …` with what blocked the last check, and `Configuration change needs a restart to apply: enabled_models adds/removes …` when the saved filter differs from the one the running daemon started with.
- `darkbloom stop`, `restart` and `start` no longer need `--force` in two states: a graceful drain with the coordinator connection down now finishes once no work is left (after a 10 s grace for a reconnect) instead of reporting a timeout at once, and shutdown waits at most 15 s for each model's unload, so a model whose native retirement never finishes no longer keeps the process from exiting.
- Policy, pending owner review: once the placement's hold has been over for ten minutes (or, without a hold, ten minutes after the selected model began waiting), and not before the placed model's minimum dwell or per-model lease ends, an adopted model outside the selection that keeps a waiting selected model out only by serving requests is released: it takes no new requests, finishes those in flight and is evicted. Holds, selected models and pins are never released.
