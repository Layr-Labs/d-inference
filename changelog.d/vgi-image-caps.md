### More images a request for Qwen and Gemma (video frames as images)

- Qwen3.5-9B, Qwen 3.5 35B, Qwen 3.6 35B and Qwen 3.8 27B take up to 64 inline images a request on one Mac, and Gemma 4 26B up to 24. Every model had a cap of 16: a Mac refused the whole request at its 17th image (413 `media_too_large`), for example a video's frames sent as images. Past 16 images a Qwen request's images share the pixels of 16 full-size images (the processor sizes each to its share), so its first-token time stays about that of 16 large images: on an M5 Ultra, 64 video frames reach first content in about 11.5 s on Qwen 3.8 27B (9.3-9.6 s for 16; 41-44 s for 64 without the budget) and about 5.5 s on the 9B. Requests with 16 or fewer images are unchanged. Super, MiMo and two-Mac pairs keep 16 (Super refuses the 17th with 415 `unsupported_media`). `DARKBLOOM_MAX_IMAGES_PER_REQUEST`, when set, is still one cap for every model.
