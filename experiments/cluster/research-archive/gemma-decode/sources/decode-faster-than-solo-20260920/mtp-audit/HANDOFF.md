## Native checks to run under the root-owned entry

Call `qualifyGemma4MTPConditioning(target:embedding:drafter:row:seedToken:hidden:check:)` with real loaded weights and an actual captured row, outside timing. Reserve its additional probe arrays and host copies. It checks six embedding IDs including vocabulary edges, 14 direct original-CBv2 draft token/hidden columns at depths 2/4/8, and the same 14 tokens plus three last-hidden columns from bounded batched `asyncEval` chains. All comparisons require byte identity. Fourteen negative controls cover wrong frontiers, seed/context, rebinding and credit bounds. It returns the assistant unbound after success. On failure, the original native owner handles the failed assistant/target lifetime; no successful qualification receipt is returned.

That probe does not qualify target MTP transactions. The other required implementation is the root-owned Gemma window-safe rectangular target adapter: no use of legacy `rollbackSpeculativeCache` at P4096 (the rotating cache ceases to be trimmable after the window fills), and no widening of the Qwen-only `CBv2TargetVerification` gate.
