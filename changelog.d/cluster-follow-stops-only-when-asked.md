### A pair being set up is no longer called stopped while the leading Mac downloads

- During `darkbloom cluster` setup, the Mac that joined ended its screen about 20 s into the leading Mac's download (or a long copy) of the chosen model with "Stopped (by `darkbloom cluster stop` on one of the Macs): the pair isn't starting any more", though nobody had stopped anything and the leading Mac's start went on (0.10.9 to 0.10.12). It took the leading Mac's status, which reads "Stopped" until both Macs' parts start, for a stop. The leading Mac now counts each stop someone asks for (`darkbloom stop`, `darkbloom cluster stop` or `darkbloom restart`, on either Mac) and its status carries the count; the Mac that joined ends only on a stop asked since the start began, and then at once instead of 20 s later. A leading Mac on an older build sends no count, so the Mac that joined follows its start to the end.
- On the leading Mac such a stop now also ends the wait while both Macs load the model, instead of a ✗ with Retry after 15 minutes, and a stop asked while the model downloads or copies keeps the pair stopped once that is done, instead of starting both Macs anyway.
