### A looping thought's answer no longer times out on a busy Mac

- When the loop guard ends a thought (Gemma 4 by default; Qwen 3.5/3.6, Nemotron 3.5 Super and GPT-OSS when their exit applies), the answer's continuation is now admitted ahead of the requests waiting in the engine's queue. On a Mac with more requests than slots it used to queue behind them and end `length` after the engine's 120 s admission lease, with no answer: 21 of 29 Gemma exits on 0.10.13's DevNet build at 12-on-8 concurrency. Under memory pressure the engine now evicts an ordinary running request before a continuation (that request recomputes its prompt). `DARKBLOOM_LOOP_EXIT_THINKING=0` still turns the exit off.
