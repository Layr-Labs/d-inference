### Cluster KV in pages

- GLM-5.3-Flash tensor-parallel ranks, GLM pipeline stages, Qwen 3.5 pipeline stages (MTP heads included), Qwen tensor-parallel shards, Gemma 4 stages (any lane count) and Nemotron 3.5 stages keep request KV in page pools instead of contiguous per-request arrays. Prefix entries share pages (refcounted, copy on write at a shared partial page) instead of copying histories, and a GLM tensor-parallel request is charged its pages (about 18.4 KB a token a rank) rather than the contiguous growth charge. Outputs are bitwise the contiguous ones. `DARKBLOOM_CLUSTER_GLM_PAGED_KV=0`, `DARKBLOOM_CLUSTER_QWEN_TP_PAGED_KV=0` and `DARKBLOOM_CLUSTER_KV_BACKEND=contiguous` keep contiguous KV for diagnosis.
- GLM tensor-parallel (solo), Qwen tensor-parallel and GLM pipeline requests start with their prompt's pages plus a 1,024-token granule and grow between steps, with no context cap; a request whose growth the memory gate refuses ends at `length` without touching others. GLM page pools compile their kernels at load and keep up to `DARKBLOOM_CLUSTER_GLM_PAGED_RETAIN_MB` (2,048 MB a rank) of slabs between requests.
