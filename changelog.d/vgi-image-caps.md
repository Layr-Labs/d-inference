### More images a request for Qwen and Gemma (video frames as images)

- Qwen3.5-9B, Qwen 3.5 35B, Qwen 3.6 35B and Qwen 3.8 27B take up to 64 inline images a request, and Gemma 4 26B up to 32; every model had a cap of 16, and a Mac refused the whole request at its 17th image with 413 `media_too_large` (a video's frames sent as images, e.g. OpenRouter's VGI-Bench traffic). Past 16 images a Qwen request's images share the pixels of 16 full-size images (each larger one is scaled down to its share), so its first-token time stays that of 16 images: on an M5 Ultra, 32 or 64 video frames reach first content in about the time 16 did (Qwen 3.8 27B 9-10 s, 9B ~4 s) instead of 2-4x longer. Requests with 16 or fewer images are unchanged. Super keeps 16. `DARKBLOOM_MAX_IMAGES_PER_REQUEST`, when set, is still one cap for every model.
