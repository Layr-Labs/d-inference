### A stale slow prefill estimate stops refusing after three deadline refusals

- A `deadline_unreachable` refusal admits nothing, so it measures nothing. An idle Mac whose only prefill measurement was slow and is now older than 120 seconds could go on refusing from it for up to 30 minutes. After three such refusals in a row with nothing admitted in between, the provider admits the next request that finds the Mac idle without a time projection, and that request's prefill replaces the estimate.
- Only refusals the Mac's own isolated prefill rate explains count: the Mac was idle and the projection priced nothing but that request's prompt. Refusals beside other work, refusals for memory or capacity, reviewed calibrations, native-media requests and native block engines (which keep their own recovery probe) are unchanged.
- A request whose deadline has already passed is still refused, and a measurement newer than 120 seconds still refuses as measured. A Mac that really is too slow admits one such request per loaded model every 120 seconds; that request can miss its first-content deadline where it would have been refused at once.
- No wire field changes. `DARKBLOOM_TTFT_ADMISSION_FIXES=0` turns this off with the other estimator corrections.
