### Inkling 2 Mini thinking off answering at once (coordinator-controlled)

- The coordinator can ask an Inkling 2 Mini request that turns thinking off, with no tools in its prompt (none declared, or `tool_choice` `none`), to answer at once with the new `inference_request` key `answer_prefill`. Its prompt then ends with an open answer message (`<|message_model|><|content_text|>`), so the model writes its answer from the first token and the reply streams as content at once, instead of after a withheld thought. The two tokens are where the next turn renders this reply, so prefix reuse is unchanged. The request's profile reports `answer_prefill_applied`.
- The provider applies its own eligibility (no images, no `logprobs`), and the new switch `DARKBLOOM_INKLING_THINKING_OFF_ANSWER_PREFILL` overrides the coordinator on this Mac: an on word answers at once for every eligible request, the local API included; an off word never does. Unset, a request without the key (the local API, an older coordinator) is unchanged: thinking off renders effort 0, at which the model may still think, and that thought is withheld.
