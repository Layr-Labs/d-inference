### A looping thought's answer no longer times out on a busy Mac

- When the loop guard ends a thought (Gemma 4 by default; Qwen 3.5/3.6, Nemotron 3.5 Super and GPT-OSS when their exit applies), the answer's continuation is now admitted ahead of the requests waiting in the engine's queue. It is also no longer refused when the Mac's serving allowance is full (the stopped request's share was taken by waiting work first: on a Mac A GPU check, the continuations were refused that way). On a Mac with more requests than slots it used to queue behind them and end `length` after the engine's 120 s admission lease, with no answer: 21 of 29 Gemma exits on 0.10.13's DevNet build at 12-on-8 concurrency. Under memory pressure the engine now evicts an ordinary running request before a continuation (that request recomputes its work so far). `DARKBLOOM_LOOP_EXIT_THINKING=0` still turns the exit off.
