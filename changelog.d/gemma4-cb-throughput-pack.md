### Gemma 4 continuous-batching Block-SDCM, zero-extra-RAM Fused QKV & Hi4/Lo4 LM head, and SAM suffix-tree MTP

- Extended opt-in Gemma 4 Block-SDCM prefill (`DARKBLOOM_GEMMA4_PREFILL_SDCM`) to continuous batching (`B >= 1`): raised default `minPromptTokens` to `3072`, added an O(N) dense-sequence safety guard (`DARKBLOOM_GEMMA4_PREFILL_SDCM_DENSITY_GUARD`) that vetoes dense low-redundancy / small-alphabet sequences onto exact chunked prefill, enabled concurrent decode alongside whole-prompt compaction (Mode A) and packed compaction cohort waves (Mode B, `DARKBLOOM_GEMMA4_PREFILL_SDCM_MAX_BATCH=8`, `DARKBLOOM_GEMMA4_PREFILL_SDCM_MAX_TOKENS=32768`), added early decode submission on mixed compaction steps, and enabled variable-top-k `Gemma4RowMappedNAX` dispatch inside Block-SDCM layers.
- Added zero-extra-RAM load-time `QKV` projection fusion (`DARKBLOOM_GEMMA4_FUSED_QKV`, default on) across non-shared Gemma 4 attention layers with zero-copy row-slice views and `ParameterUpdateObserving` invalidation across all model/layer wrappers.
- Added opt-in zero-extra-RAM `Hi4/Lo4` bit-plane sparse LM-head sampling (`Gemma4Hi4Lo4Embedding`, `DARKBLOOM_GEMMA4_HI4_LM_HEAD=off|refine|exact`, default `off`) for 8-bit group-64 affine tied Gemma 4 embeddings: `refine` screens with native 4-bit `hiWeight` and refines top-64 candidates at exact 8-bit precision across all GPU cores; `exact` enforces certified 0-ULP Cauchy-Schwarz bounds with zero-host-sync GPU dense fallback.
- Added opt-in zero-draft-cost CPU online suffix-automaton (`SAM`) speculative drafter (`CBv2SuffixTreeDrafter`, `DARKBLOOM_CBV2_SUFFIX_MTP=1`, default off) supporting `B = 1..16` with per-row prefix commits, bit-exact greedy/stochastic verification, bounded cold-prompt indexing, and composition with compacted Block-SDCM paged rows.
- Limitations: Block-SDCM remains opt-in (default `off`) and Mode B packs equal-length cohorts; `Hi4/Lo4` sparse LM-head applies only to 8-bit group-64 affine tied Gemma 4 embeddings and falls back to dense projection when penalties, grammars, or logprobs are requested; `DARKBLOOM_CBV2_SUFFIX_MTP` is opt-in (default `off`) and yields speedups primarily on code, RAG, and multi-turn editing streams with repeating n-grams.
