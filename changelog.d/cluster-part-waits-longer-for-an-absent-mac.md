### A pair's leading Mac starts its part less often while the other Mac is away

- While the other Mac of a pair doesn't answer (asleep, off, its cable out), the leading Mac keeps its part ready for 10 minutes so the pair re-forms when that Mac is back. Its part can't form the pair meanwhile, and each start reads its share of the model and registers with the coordinator again. A part that ended was started again every 30 seconds (every 48 seconds for a MiMo leader whose other Mac slept). It now starts again after 30 seconds, then 1, 2 and 4 minutes, twice as long each time up to 10 minutes, and at once when the other Mac answers. While the other Mac answers, a part that keeps ending starts again as before (6, 12, 24, then every 30 seconds).
- `darkbloom cluster status` on the leading Mac says when, under the line that it waits for the other Mac: ``This Mac's part starts again in 4 min, or as soon as the other Mac answers: while the other Mac is away, each start waits twice as long as the last, up to 10 min.``
