### GLM pair: speculative depth can follow load without changing outputs

- A GLM-5.3-Flash tensor-parallel pair can verify one NextN proposal a round while three or more requests decode together and two otherwise (`DARKBLOOM_GLM_TP_MTP_DEPTH_POLICY=load`, threshold `DARKBLOOM_GLM_TP_MTP_LOAD_LANES`, 2 through 8, default 3), so a busy pair verifies fewer rows per accepted token. Every request keeps the tokens and logits it gets alone: two- and three-row verification windows give each row the same bits on the pair, and the policy never verifies a window of one row.
- `DARKBLOOM_GLM_TP_WIDTH_INVARIANT=1` (implied by `load`) gives every verification row one arithmetic at any window width: a request's last round at its output limit verifies its token beside a discarded padding row, a window straddling the dense-to-sparse attention switch at 2,048 tokens projects its attention rows in one product, and four-row windows (depth 3) absorb their attention queries in products of at most three rows, so depth 3 matches depths 1 and 2 too. Both switches are off by default, with today's outputs bit for bit; both Macs' cluster services must set the same values (the owner forwards them to its worker and both ranks bind them into their load agreement).
