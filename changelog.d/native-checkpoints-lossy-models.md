### GPT-OSS, GLM-5.3-Flash and Bonsai 2 prompt caches now restore exactly

- A cached prompt on GPT-OSS 20B, GLM-5.3-Flash and Ternary Bonsai 2 (27B and 9B) now gets the answer the same prompt gets without the cache. These models now save their prompt checkpoints at native precision. Before, they used the provider-wide `balanced` default, which stores older attention rows at 4 bits: on an M5 Ultra, GLM's restored answers parted from the cold ones in 2 of 4 multi-turn tool prompts, each time at a near-tie; with native checkpoints GLM gave the cold answer, and restores were no slower.
- Checkpoints are larger on disk: GPT-OSS about ×3 (24 KB instead of 8 KB per token), GLM ×1.1 to ×1.6, Bonsai ×1.3 to ×3. The SSD cache keeps its shared disk budget (half the free space), its 20 GiB free-space floor and its daily write cap, so it never fills a disk; on a nearly full Mac older checkpoints are evicted sooner.
- A per-model setting for one of these models or the process switch still selects another precision; the global setting no longer applies to them. Existing checkpoints of these models stop matching after the update and age out within their 30-minute lifetime. Gemma 4 26B QAT keeps `balanced` checkpoints for now.
