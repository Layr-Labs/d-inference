### Deadline admission estimators

- Charge decode in first-token deadline projections once per batch step, priced from the step shape the decode rate was measured at (rows, confirmed tokens and draft width per step), instead of summing every running row's tokens against a per-request rate. An MTP round is priced by the tokens it was measured confirming rather than its full draft width. Rows the live MTP planner cannot speculate (beyond its batch gate, or with no depth left inside the automatic verification envelope) are charged their one target token instead of the full draft width, no row is charged more depth than that envelope allows, and a full-slot MTP stretch with no live depth is projected in one jump like plain decode. Requests carrying a reviewed deadline calibration keep the per-row charge its error envelope was measured against.
- Stop one slow "isolated" prefill sample from locking an ordinary engine out of deadline admission until restart. Admission never prices below one eighth of the startup calibration's idle rate, and isolated evidence that expired while the engine was idle at nominal posture is priced at no less than that rate. Evidence older than 30 minutes on an idle engine is treated as absent, with or without a calibrated rate, so a calibration slowed by another process cannot lock admission either. Published rates are unchanged.
- Keep measurement age and expiry tied to the newest observation when older completed work arrives later, and stop a late older cold sample from restarting the combined prefill estimate.
- `DARKBLOOM_TTFT_ADMISSION_FIXES=0` restores the previous estimators. `darkbloom start` forwards it to the background provider, so the rollback also reaches launchd-managed providers.
