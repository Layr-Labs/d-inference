### Grammar-aware MTP

- Adopt SDK #409: constrained rows draft with MTP and every committed position is verified under the row's grammar. On by default since 0.10.13 for the measured Qwen 3.8 27B build (`EigenLabs/Qwen3.8-27B-4bit-mtp`), where constraint spans are on (measured together), and for GLM-5.3-Flash (`EigenLabs/GLM-5.3-Flash-MLX-NVFP4-mtp`), whose native engine now verifies constrained drafts too; Gemma 4 and Super keep target-only constrained rows unless `DARKBLOOM_CBV2_MTP_GRAMMAR=1`. `DARKBLOOM_CBV2_MTP_GRAMMAR=0` (or `DARKBLOOM_CONSTRAINT_SPANS=0`) restores 0.10.12's target-only constrained rows. Forwarded by the LaunchAgent.
