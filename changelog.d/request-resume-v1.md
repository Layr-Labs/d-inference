### A request's work survives its coordinator's death

- The provider registers `request_resume_protocol: 1`. When a coordinator puts a resume id in a request's sealed body (`_darkbloom_resume_id`) and the connection to it drops, the provider keeps that request's generation and output for 15 s after the disconnect, or after the generation's end when that is later, and lists the id in its next `register` (`resumable_requests`, at most 64). Every other in-flight request is cancelled at the disconnect, as before.
- A surviving coordinator's dispatch of the same request takes the whole answer when its decrypted body has the same SHA-256 (compared in constant time), its session key differs, its outer cache and prompt fields are equal, it carries no receipt nonce, it is not a pair or cluster request, and, when the build pins coordinator request-signing keys, its signature verifies. It gets every chunk from the first, sealed to its own session key under its own request id, then the terminal with the whole usage and `resumed: true`. Any other dispatch runs as new; a held request that nothing takes is cancelled at the end of the window, unpaid.
