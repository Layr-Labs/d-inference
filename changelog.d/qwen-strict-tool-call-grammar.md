### Strict Qwen tool calls always match their schemas

- On Qwen 3.8 27B, Qwen 3.6 35B and Qwen 3.5 9B and 35B, a tool call to a function declared `strict: true` could miss the caller's schema (a quoted number, a wrong enum value, a missing required argument, a value inside a `$ref` definition of the wrong JSON type); the provider checks such a call when it closes, after the answer started streaming, and the response then failed partway. On one Mac the provider now constrains these calls while they are sampled: only a declared function, its declared parameters (each at most once, every required one), and values of the declared types and enums can be written, in the template's call format. Reasoning and text around the calls, the choice whether to call, the prompt and the streamed call deltas are unchanged.
- It applies to `tool_choice` `auto` (or absent) requests that declare at least one `strict` function; every call of such a request is held to its schema, as the check already holds it. Local `$ref`/`$defs` are inlined, `anyOf` of a type and `null` is supported, and a request whose schemas use a keyword the grammar cannot hold exactly (`pattern`, `minimum`, `minLength`, unions of several types, recursive references, ...) is served unconstrained, as before, never refused. Cluster pairs keep the previous behavior.
- A constrained answer that runs alone on its Mac decodes without MTP drafting (as every constrained request does).
- `DARKBLOOM_STRICT_TOOL_GRAMMAR=off` turns it off on a Mac; a list of model types or model-id parts (`qwen3.8-27b,qwen3_5_moe`) limits it to those builds. `DARKBLOOM_STRICT_TOOL_GRAMMAR_ALL_TOOLS=on` also constrains the calls of requests without `strict` functions. Both reach a provider started by `darkbloom start`.
