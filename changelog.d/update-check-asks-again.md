### The update check asks again instead of waiting a minute, and says what failed

- The check for a new release sent one request to the coordinator and waited up to 60 seconds for its answer. When that connection stalled, `darkbloom update` failed after a minute with "download failed: update check failed: The request timed out", although nothing had been downloaded, and `--check-only` with "update check failed: …". Now each try waits at most 15 seconds, and a try that gets no answer, loses or can't open its connection, or can't resolve the coordinator's name is made again after about 1, then 2 seconds: 3 tries, under a minute in all. An answer from the coordinator, an error included, is not asked again. Every automatic update (a single Mac's, a pair's, the watchdog's and the startup check) uses the same check, so one stalled request no longer costs a pair its update slot.
- A check that still fails says so plainly, on two lines: `Couldn't check for updates: api.darkbloom.dev didn't answer within 15 s (3 tries). Nothing was downloaded or changed.` and ``Run `darkbloom update` again in a minute. If it keeps failing, check this Mac's network connection.``
